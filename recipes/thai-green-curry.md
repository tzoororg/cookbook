## Ingredients

### For the curry

- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 4-6 tbsp | thai green curry paste
- 250 ml | chicken stock, low sodium
- 400 g | coconut milk, full fat
- 1-3 tsp | fish sauce
- 1-3 tsp | white sugar
- 1/8 tsp | salt
- 6 | kaffir lime leaves, torn in half
- 350 g | chicken thigh, boneless skinless, sliced
- 2 | japanese eggplants, small, in 1cm slices
- 1.5 cup | snow peas, trimmed
- 16 | thai basil leaves
- 1/2 | lime, juice only

### Extra, if using jarred paste

- 2 | garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp | ginger, finely grated
- 1 tbsp | lemongrass paste

### To serve

- | crispy fried asian shallots
- | thai basil or cilantro
- | green or red chili, sliced
- | jasmine rice, steamed

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a heavy skillet or pot over medium-high heat.
2. Add the curry paste (plus the garlic, ginger and lemongrass if using jarred paste) and cook 2–3 minutes until it mostly dries out. Don't breathe in the fumes.
3. Add the stock and coconut milk and stir to dissolve the paste.
4. Season: with jarred paste use 1 tsp fish sauce, 1 tsp sugar and no salt. With homemade paste use 3 tsp fish sauce, 3 tsp sugar and the salt.
5. Add the lime leaves, stir and bring to a simmer.
6. Add the chicken, lower to a gentle bubble and cook 7 minutes.
7. Add the eggplant and cook 5 minutes until soft.
8. Taste and adjust — fish sauce or salt for savouriness, sugar for sweetness.
9. Add the snow peas and cook 2 minutes, then stir through the basil and lime juice. The sauce stays on the thin side; stop simmering here.
10. Serve over jasmine rice with the garnishes.
