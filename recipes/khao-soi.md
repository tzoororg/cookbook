## Ingredients

### For the curry

- 2 tbsp | coconut oil
- 4 tbsp | khao soi curry paste
- 400 ml | coconut milk
- 500 ml | chicken stock
- 2 | chicken legs
- 2 tbsp | fish sauce
- 1 tbsp | palm sugar

### To serve

- 400 g | fresh egg noodles
- | pickled mustard greens
- 3 | shallots, thinly sliced
- 2 | limes, in wedges
- | chili oil

## Steps

1. Warm the coconut oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Fry the curry paste for 3 minutes until fragrant and the oil separates.
2. Add half the coconut milk and simmer, stirring, until thickened and glossy.
3. Add the chicken, stock, and remaining coconut milk. Simmer gently for 40 minutes until the chicken is tender.
4. Season with fish sauce and palm sugar. Taste — it should be rich, salty, faintly sweet.
5. Boil the noodles; fry a handful crisp in oil for topping.
6. Serve noodles in bowls, ladle over curry and chicken, top with crispy noodles, shallots, pickled greens, lime, and chili oil.

## Notes

The crispy noodle topping is not optional — it is the whole texture of the dish.
Pickled mustard greens keep for months in the fridge once opened.
