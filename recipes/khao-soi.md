## Ingredients

### For the khao soi paste

- 2 | thai bird's eye chilies
- 2 | shallots, medium
- 6 | garlic cloves
- 1 inch | ginger, peeled and sliced
- 1/4 cup | cilantro, stems and leaves
- 1 | lime, zest only
- 2 tsp | ground turmeric
- 2 tsp | ground coriander
- 1 tsp | curry powder
- 2 tbsp | shrimp paste

### For the soup

- 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
- 450 g | chicken thighs, boneless skinless, sliced
- 2 tbsp | thai red curry paste
- 1 l | chicken stock, low sodium
- 2 tsp | brown sugar
- 400 ml | coconut milk, unsweetened
- 3 tbsp | fish sauce
- 450 g | fresh egg noodles, thick

### To garnish

- | shallots, thinly sliced
- | lime wedges
- | pickled mustard greens
- | crispy fried noodles
- | cilantro, chopped
- | thai chili paste (nam prik pao)

## Steps

1. Blend all the paste ingredients in a food processor until smooth.
2. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Stir-fry the chicken until browned, then remove and set aside.
3. Fry the paste in the remaining fat for 3–5 minutes until fragrant. Add the red curry paste, stock and brown sugar, and bring to a boil.
4. Lower the heat to a gentle simmer, stir in the coconut milk and fish sauce, and return the chicken to the pot.
5. Cook the noodles according to the packet.
6. Divide the noodles between four bowls, ladle over the chicken and broth, and top with sliced shallots, lime wedges, pickled mustard greens, crispy noodles, cilantro and chili paste.

## Notes

Untested — added from the source above on 2026-08-13.

The crispy noodle topping is the whole texture of the dish; fry a handful of the raw
egg noodles in oil until they puff. Shrimp paste can be swapped for laksa paste.
