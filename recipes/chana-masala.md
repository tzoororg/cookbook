## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp | neutral oil
- 1 | yellow onion, finely diced
- 3 | garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 1-2 | green chilies, split
- 2 tsp | ground cumin
- 1 tsp | ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp | turmeric
- 1 tsp | garam masala
- 400 g | crushed tomatoes
- 480 g | chickpeas, drained
- 1 tsp | amchur
- | cilantro, a big handful

## Steps

1. Heat the oil and fry the onion over medium heat for 10 minutes until it is properly brown, not just soft.
2. Add the garlic, ginger and chilies; cook 2 minutes until the raw smell is gone.
3. Add the ground spices and stir for 30 seconds, then the tomatoes. Cook down until the oil separates at the edges.
4. Add the chickpeas and a splash of water. Simmer 15 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas against the side to thicken.
5. Off the heat, stir in the amchur and garam masala. Finish with cilantro.

## Notes

Skipping the long onion fry is the single biggest reason home chana masala tastes thin.
Amchur (dried mango powder) can be replaced with a squeeze of lemon, but it is not the same.
