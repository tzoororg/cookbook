## Ingredients

### For the dal

- 95 g | masoor dal, red lentils
- 2 tbsp | moong dal, optional
- 2 | tomatoes, deseeded and chopped
- 1 | green chili, optional
- 350 ml | water

### For the tempering

- 1-2 tbsp | ghee, or 1 tbsp oil
- 1 | dried red chili, broken
- 2 | garlic cloves, chopped
- 1/4 tsp | mustard seeds, optional
- 1/2 tsp | cumin seeds
- 1 pinch | asafoetida (hing)
- 1/2 tsp | salt
- 1/4 tsp | kashmiri red chili powder
- 1/4 tsp | turmeric
- 1/8 tsp | garam masala

### To finish

- 1/2 tsp | kasuri methi, dried fenugreek leaves
- 2 tbsp | coriander leaves, finely chopped
- 1-2 tbsp | lemon juice

## Steps

1. Put the lentils in a pot or pressure cooker and rinse several times until the water runs clear.
2. Add the tomatoes, water and green chili.
3. Pressure cook for 1–2 whistles on medium heat. On the stovetop, bring to a boil, then simmer covered until the lentils are soft, skimming the froth.
4. Once the pressure drops, open the cooker and mash the dal to the consistency you want — smooth or coarse. Loosen with a few tablespoons of hot water if needed.
5. Heat the ghee in a small pan over medium heat. Add the mustard seeds, cumin seeds, dried chili and garlic.
6. Sauté until the garlic turns lightly golden. Take off the heat and add the asafoetida, chili powder, turmeric and garam masala.
7. Pour the tempering straight into the dal.
8. Add the salt and crushed kasuri methi, stir, and simmer 2 minutes. Taste and adjust the salt.
9. Squeeze over the lemon juice, garnish with coriander, and serve with rice or roti.

## Notes

Untested — added from the source above on 2026-08-13.

The tempering goes in off the heat so the ground spices don't burn; pour it in while it's
still sizzling. Red lentils need no soaking and are soft in about 15 minutes on the stovetop.
