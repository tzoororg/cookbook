## Ingredients

### Rice

- 300 g | glutinous rice
- 250 ml | coconut milk
- 60 g | sugar
- 1/2 tsp | salt

### To serve

- 2 | ripe mangoes
- 80 ml | coconut milk
- 1 tbsp | sugar
- | toasted sesame seeds

## Steps

1. Soak the glutinous rice in cold water for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight. Drain.
2. Steam the rice over simmering water for 25 minutes, lined with muslin, until translucent and tender.
3. Warm the coconut milk with the sugar and salt until dissolved — do not boil it.
4. Pour the warm coconut milk over the hot rice, stir once, cover, and leave 20 minutes to absorb.
5. Make the topping: warm the remaining coconut milk with sugar and a pinch of salt until slightly thickened.
6. Serve the rice at room temperature with sliced mango, the coconut topping, and sesame seeds.
