## Ingredients

- 500 g | spinach
- 250 g | paneer, cubed
- 2 tbsp | ghee
- 1 | yellow onion, finely diced
- 4 | garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp | ginger, grated
- 2 | green chilies
- 1 tsp | ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp | garam masala
- 60 ml | cream
- 1-2 tsp | lemon juice
- | salt

## Steps

1. Blanch the spinach for 60 seconds, then drop it straight into ice water to hold the colour.
2. Blend the spinach with the chilies to a coarse purée — not a smooth one.
3. Fry the paneer in half the ghee until golden on two sides, then set aside in warm salted water so it stays soft.
4. In the rest of the ghee, cook the onion until soft, then the garlic, ginger and cumin.
5. Add the spinach purée and cook 5 minutes. Stir in the cream, garam masala, paneer and lemon juice.
